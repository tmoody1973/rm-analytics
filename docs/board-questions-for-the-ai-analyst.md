# Questions the board can ask the AI analyst

> A starter set for Radio Milwaukee board members using the data analyst at
> [data.radiomilwaukee.org](https://data.radiomilwaukee.org). Ask in plain language —
> the assistant pulls live numbers from the warehouse and cites where each one came
> from. It won't invent figures; if it can't answer, it says so.
>
> Tip: every question is a starting point. Follow up. "Why?", "compared to last year?",
> "which brand?", and "what should we do about it?" all work.

## The money — are we healthy and on plan?

- How are we tracking against budget this fiscal year?
- What's our revenue year-to-date, and how does it compare to the same point last year?
- Where is revenue coming from this year — individual giving, foundations, underwriting?
- Are we running a surplus or a deficit year-to-date?
- How many months of operating cash do we have on hand?
- Is our spending on budget, or are expenses running ahead of plan?

## Membership and giving — is our donor base strong?

- How many active donors do we have, and what's their total lifetime giving?
- How are we doing on member retention this year — are we keeping last year's donors?
- How many sustaining (monthly) members do we have, and what's the recurring revenue from them?
- Are we acquiring new donors faster than we're losing them?
- What share of our giving comes from a small number of major gifts versus the broad base?
- Where are our donors located?

## Audience and reach — who are we reaching, and is it growing?

- How many people are we reaching across broadcast and streaming?
- How is 88Nine's streaming audience trending this year?
- How does HYFIN compare to 88Nine — is it growing?
- What does our Nielsen standing in the Milwaukee market look like?
- When are people actually listening — what are our busiest times of day?
- Are listeners staying longer or tuning out faster than they used to?

## Digital and marketing — how are we doing online?

- How is traffic to our website trending?
- How do people find us — search, social, email, direct?
- What stories or pages are drawing the most people right now?
- How are our newsletters performing, and which topics get the most opens?
- Where is our web audience located?

## How we compare — are we keeping up?

- How does our social engagement compare to other public radio stations we track?
- What kinds of content are working for our competitors that we aren't doing?
- Are we punching above or below our weight relative to peers our size?

## The mission — connecting numbers to impact

- Did our spring pledge drive move the needle on streaming and giving?
- Which brand is reaching the most new people this year?
- If you had to tell the board one thing that's going well and one thing to watch, what would they be?

## Good questions to ask *about* an answer

- What's the source and time period for that number?
- Is that a meaningful change, or could it be noise / a small sample?
- How does that compare to last year, or to our goal?
- Which brand or segment is driving that?

## What it can't answer yet (and why)

The assistant is honest about gaps. As of now it will tell you these aren't tracked
in the warehouse:

- **Donor identities** — the giving data is de-identified by design (no names, emails,
  or phone numbers). It can analyze *patterns* and *de-identified records*, but it
  cannot tell you *who* a specific donor is, or who gave a particular gift.
- **Underwriting pipeline** — underwriting *revenue* is in finance, but the sales
  pipeline, individual sponsors, and contracts aren't loaded yet.
- **Events and grants pipeline** — ticketing, attendance, and the grants pipeline
  aren't in the warehouse yet.
- **Deep historical finance** — only the current fiscal year's finance figures are
  loaded, so multi-year financial trends aren't available yet.

If you ask one of these, the assistant will say so plainly and offer the closest
thing it *can* answer — rather than guess.
