# Getting into the Radio Milwaukee Data Dashboard

Welcome! This is a quick guide to signing in to the Radio Milwaukee analytics
dashboard. It takes about two minutes, and you only have to do it once.

## Before you start

We've added your email address to the guest list so you can get in. **Please use
the exact email address Radio Milwaukee invited** — the one this guide was sent
to. If you sign in with a different email, the system won't recognize you.

## Steps

1. Go to **[data.radiomilwaukee.org](https://data.radiomilwaukee.org)** in your
   web browser (Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox all work).

2. Click **Sign in**.

3. Enter your email address — again, the **exact** one Radio Milwaukee used to
   invite you.

4. Check your inbox. You'll get a short verification email or code from us to
   confirm it's really you. Enter the code (or click the link) to continue.

5. That's it — you'll land on the dashboard. It opens on the **Start Here** tab,
   which walks you through what you're looking at and the kinds of questions you
   can ask.

## If you see an "Access restricted" screen

That almost always means you signed in with a *different* email than the one on
the invite. Two things to try:

- Sign out and sign back in using the exact email Radio Milwaukee invited.
- Still stuck? Email **Tarik at tarik@radiomilwaukee.org** and he'll get you
  sorted out.

## Coming back later

You don't need to repeat any of this. Just visit
**[data.radiomilwaukee.org](https://data.radiomilwaukee.org)** and you'll be
signed in. If you're on a shared or public computer, sign out when you're done.

Questions any time: **tarik@radiomilwaukee.org**.
